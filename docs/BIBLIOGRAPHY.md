## Facts vs opinions in the course

- **Fact, machine-checked:** kana and romaji agree on every word.
- **Fact, checked against JMdict:** word spellings, readings, and meanings.
- **Fact, from general knowledge, not yet checked:** media years and Japanese titles, and listed on and kun readings.
- **Opinion from guide sites:** the Study Guide's advice. Where they disagree, the guide says so.
- **Teaching convention:** typed romaji, and the order of lessons.
- **Generalisations:** gestures and etiquette notes describe common customs, not universal rules.
- **Opinion:** which media suits which stage, and the kanji memory hooks.

No official JLPT vocabulary or kanji lists are used. N5 content here reflects common teaching practice.
