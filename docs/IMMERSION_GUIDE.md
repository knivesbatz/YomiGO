# Everyday immersion

Small and daily beats big and rare. Add one step at a time, and move on once it feels normal.

**From day one**
- Greet your household in Japanese.
- Listen to any Japanese for a few minutes, without trying to understand it. You're learning the rhythm.

**Once you know hiragana**
- Put sticky notes on things at home.
- Read kana wherever you find it: game menus, song titles, and web pages.

**Once you know katakana**
- Set your phone keyboard to Japanese, and type a shopping list.
- Play a kana-only game such as an early Dragon Quest or Pokémon.

**While learning grammar**
- Narrate your day: パンを食べます.
- Watch one scene several times instead of a whole film once. Use Japanese subtitles if you can.
- Shadow a short line: listen, then say it along with the speaker.

**Once you've met some kanji**
- Set your phone's language to Japanese for a day, and see how far you get.
- Read NHK Easy News or a Tadoku free reader.
- Use Reader in your browser, and save words you meet twice.

**Do it together.** With two of you, try one meal a week with Japanese only, however simple.

**Passive and active both count.** Background listening builds your ear, but you learn the most when you're paying attention. Aim for some of each daily.
