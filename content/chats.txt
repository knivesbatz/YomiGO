# Scripted offline conversations. Each `say` is what the character says, each `want` lists accepted replies.
@chat greet1 | Morning at the station | 朝の駅 | はな
say: おはようございます！ | ohayou gozaimasu! | Good morning!
want: おはようございます;おはよう | Reply with the morning greeting.
say: さようなら！ | sayounara! | Goodbye!
want: さようなら | Say goodbye back.

@chat thanks1 | Dropped umbrella | 傘 | けん

want: ありがとうございます;ありがとう | Thank them.
say: どういたしまして。 | dou itashimashite. | You're welcome.
want: さようなら;すみません;ありがとうございます | Close the exchange: another thanks or a goodbye.
